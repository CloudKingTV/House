# Welcome speech

Delivered at the Welcome Dinner on **Sat 14 Nov**, after everyone is seated and before the first course. **About 3 minutes.** Say it; don't read it. Adjust it to your own voice.

---

Good evening, and welcome to SagaDAO House.

Before anything else: thank you for being here. Every person at this table was invited for a reason. You're here because of what you build and what you share, and because we think you're the people others at Breakpoint will want to meet this week.

A year ago we did this in Dubai. It was fun and we learned a lot. The biggest lesson was that a house is only as good as what happens inside it. So this week is different.

This week, **this house is a stage.** Every morning, founders will sit at this table over breakfast and argue about what's next. Every evening, we'll host sessions people choose over everything else happening in London. And in the room next door there's a studio where we'll record the conversations that usually happen in a corridor and disappear.

You're not guests watching that happen. **You're the hosts.** That's what the charter was about. It isn't a contract so much as an agreement that we build this week together. What you post, the conversations you start, and the people you bring in make this house matter.

In return, our job is to look after you. That means cars when you need them, a team that knows your name and your coffee order, and a card on your pillow every night so you never wake up wondering what's happening. If anything isn't right, tell [House manager]. It's their whole job this week to make it right.

A few people to thank: [Presenting partner] — [one sincere line]. And our partners: [names]. They're not here because of logos. They're here because they believe in what gets built in rooms like this.

So: have a great week. Meet the people you came to meet. Say something worth clipping. Get some sleep occasionally.

To SagaDAO House, London.
