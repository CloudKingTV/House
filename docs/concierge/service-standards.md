# Service standards

The difference between a nice house and a premium one is how people are treated. Everyone working the house — team, staff, crew and vendors — reads this page.

## The ten standards

1. **Use names.** Learn every resident's name before they arrive. Greet partners and speakers by name at the door.
2. **Never say "I don't know".** Say "Let me find out" and come back within 15 minutes.
3. **One request, one owner.** Whoever receives a request owns it until it's done, even if someone else does the task.
4. **Anticipate.** It's raining, so there are umbrellas at the door. It's late, so cars are waiting. Guests have been at Olympia all day, so there's hot food and tea when they get in.
5. **Remember preferences.** Their coffee order is made without asking by day two.
6. **Respond within 15 minutes** on WhatsApp between 07:00 and 01:00.
7. **Keep shared spaces photo-ready.** Clear dishes, straighten cushions and remove clutter before every session.
8. **Be quiet about guests.** Nothing about residents' plans, habits or holdings goes outside the house.
9. **Security is hospitality.** Checks happen warmly and firmly.
10. **Close the loop.** Every issue ends with "Done. Anything else?"

## Response times

| Request | Target |
|---|---|
| WhatsApp message | 15 min |
| Something missing from a room | 30 min |
| Car needed now | 20 min |
| Dietary issue at a meal | Before the plate leaves the kitchen |
| Something broken (Wi-Fi, heating, shower) | 30 min to acknowledge; fixed or a workaround within 2 h |

## Small touches that make it premium
- A card on the pillow each night with tomorrow's schedule
- Hot drinks and blankets by the door on cold evenings
- Phone chargers in every room and every car
- Fresh flowers in the entrance, replaced mid-week
- A tray with water and something small in each room at turndown
- Birthdays and milestones (from the preference form) marked quietly at dinner
