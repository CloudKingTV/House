# House and venue

## The model: a private house plus a venue

A 5-bedroom house can't host 300 people, and most London lets prohibit parties anyway. So the model is settled (see the [decisions log](decisions-log.md)):

- **The house is private.** Residents, the studio, breakfasts for up to about 16, and resident dinners. Getting in is by invitation, which is what makes it feel special.
- **The venue is public.** The Saga Sessions, Demo Hour and the flagship night happen at a licensed venue near Olympia.

## Flagship night: the minimum-spend buyout

On this budget, the smartest venue route in London is a **minimum-spend private hire**: a bar, restaurant or members' club closes to the public for the night, and instead of a hire fee you commit to spending £X on food and drink. The bar money you would spend anyway becomes the venue.

- Ask for the **minimum spend on a Monday or Tuesday night** during Breakpoint week. Those nights are usually cheaper than Thursday to Saturday, but every side event will be asking too.
- Confirm what counts toward the minimum (usually food and drink, not service charge) and whether the venue holds a **premises licence** (then no TEN is needed).
- Check capacity. 300 people across the night isn't 300 at once; ask for peak capacity.
- **Alternative:** a partner hosts it at their own venue, and we bring the programme, the door and the content. That brings the venue line close to £0.

## Venue criteria (sessions and flagship night)

- [ ] Capacity of 300–400 standing, plus a session layout of 80–150 seated
- [ ] Holds a **premises licence** (so no TEN is needed) or is TEN-friendly
- [ ] In-house AV or AV-friendly, with power for a stream
- [ ] A **door and arrival zone** big enough for the check-in moment, cloakroom and photo wall. It will be November in London, so plan for coats and umbrellas.
- [ ] Within 10 minutes of Olympia

**Sessions on this budget:** the £5k venue line can't pay for three separate hires. Options, from cheapest up:
1. **A partner's space.** Many crypto companies and funds in London have offices or event floors; hosting a Saga Session is a natural fit for a Programme Partner.
2. **The flagship venue at off-peak hours.** Ask the buyout venue for an early-evening or daytime rate on the other nights.
3. **A private room in a restaurant or pub** for sessions of 40–80, again on a minimum spend.

**Bigger-ticket venues** (e.g. the Design Museum or Leighton House) only make sense if a partner pays for them.
