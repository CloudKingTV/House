# House and venue

## The key decision

Twelve people sleeping in a house and 300+ people coming to events are **two different jobs**. Most London residential lets, especially short lets and Airbnb-style properties, prohibit events and parties outright, and neighbours in W8/W14 will complain about noise.

Choose one of three options by **Fri 2 Oct** and log it in the [decisions log](decisions-log.md).

| Option | What it is | Upside | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. One event-grade house** | A large townhouse or mansion let **explicitly for events**, with written permission | One address that becomes *the* place | Rare, expensive; capacity and neighbours limit numbers |
| **B. Residence + nearby venue** | A premium house for 12, plus a separate venue for sessions and the flagship night | Legal, scalable and calm for residents | Two sites to run; you have to make the venue feel like "the House" |
| **C. Hybrid** | Intimate programming at the house (breakfasts, studio, roundtables, dinners) and the big nights at a venue | Keeps the house exclusive while the venue handles scale | More logistics, but this is the premium model |

**Recommendation: C.** Keeping the house for small, invited moments makes getting in feel special, which is exactly what Dubai lacked. The 300+ programme and the party go to a venue that is licensed, insured and built for it.

## Residence criteria

- [ ] 12 beds in **at least 8–10 bedrooms**, with no more than two sharing, and ideally ensuite
- [ ] Within **15 minutes' walk or 10 minutes' drive of Olympia**. Good areas: Addison Road, Holland Park, Brook Green, Kensington (W8/W14), Notting Hill
- [ ] A room that can become the **studio** (quiet, controllable light, 3 × 4 m or larger)
- [ ] A dining table (or two) seating 14–16 for the welcome dinner
- [ ] A reception room for 25–40 for breakfasts and roundtables
- [ ] Wired or strong fibre Wi-Fi; ask for a speed test screenshot
- [ ] **Written permission** for the use you plan (filming, 40-person gatherings)
- [ ] Off-street pickup and drop-off, or a quiet street for cars
- [ ] Storage for kit, gifts and crew gear
- [ ] Clear cancellation, damage-deposit and cleaning terms

## Venue criteria (options B and C)

- [ ] Capacity of 300–400 standing, plus a session layout of 80–150 seated
- [ ] Holds a **premises licence** (so no TEN is needed) or is TEN-friendly
- [ ] In-house AV or AV-friendly, with power for a stream
- [ ] A **door and arrival zone** big enough for the check-in moment, cloakroom and photo wall. It will be November in London, so plan for coats and umbrellas.
- [ ] Within 10 minutes of Olympia

**Places worth an enquiry near Olympia:** Design Museum (Kensington High Street), Leighton House (Holland Park), private members' clubs in Kensington and Notting Hill, gallery spaces, and hotel event floors on Kensington High Street. Check availability and pricing for all of them; Breakpoint week will be busy.
