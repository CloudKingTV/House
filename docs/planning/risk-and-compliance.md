# Risk and compliance

This is not legal advice. Check anything that affects licensing or insurance with the council and your broker.

## Licensing (England)

- **Temporary Event Notice (TEN):** needed if you sell alcohol, provide regulated entertainment or serve late-night refreshment at a place that has no premises licence.
  - Maximum **499 people at any one time**, including staff, for up to 168 hours.
  - A standard TEN needs **at least 10 clear working days' notice** (the submission day and the event day don't count). A late TEN needs 5–9 working days and is refused outright if anyone objects.
  - The police and environmental health can object within 3 working days. **Submit early (target 23 Oct).**
  - Fee: £21. Apply to the council where the property sits: **Hammersmith & Fulham** (Olympia side) or **Kensington & Chelsea**, depending on the address.
- **Free alcohol isn't automatically exempt.** If alcohol is included in any ticket or sponsor package, treat it as a sale. The simplest route is a **licensed bar company** that operates under its own licence or TEN. Confirm with the council.
- **Security:** anyone doing door supervision at a licensed event must hold an **SIA licence**. Hire through an SIA-approved contractor.

## Property

- **Written permission** from the landlord or host for every event, and for filming, before anything is announced.
- Record the inventory and condition on arrival, with photos of every room. Record them again at checkout.
- **Neighbour letter:** deliver it by 6 Nov with dates, a contact number and a small gift. It is courteous and it prevents complaints.

## Insurance

- **Event public liability:** £5m minimum; many venues require £10m.
- Employer's liability if you engage staff directly.
- Check whether the production crew carries its own equipment insurance.

## Personal security for residents

Crypto guests are visible and well known, and physical-coercion attacks on holders have increased. Treat this seriously and quietly.

- **Never post the house address or a live location.** Post house content with a delay, and never show the street, the front door or the house number.
- Keep street signs, number plates and landmarks near the house out of shot.
- Residents get a **door code or key only for themselves**. No unregistered overnight guests.
- Overnight security presence on event nights.
- **All staff and crew are vetted**, with names on file and no plus-ones.
- Remind residents not to discuss holdings or wallets on camera or in public, and not to leave devices unlocked in shared spaces.
- Event guest lists are checked by name at the door. No "I'm with the DJ".

## Health and safety

- A first-aid-trained person on site at every event.
- Fire exits clear and marked, with capacity respected.
- **Allergies and medical notes** from preference forms go to the caterer and house manager, and to no one else.
- Tell the caterer about any dietary requirements 72 hours before each meal.
- Accessibility: ask every resident and speaker about access needs, and choose venues with step-free options where possible.

## Data and privacy

- Keep personal data (flights, dietary, medical, contact details) only in this private repo or the tools we control. Delete medical notes after the event.
- Use a **photo and filming consent** line in the charter and in the attendee Luma registration. Offer a no-photo lanyard or wristband colour.
