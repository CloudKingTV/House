# Vision: SagaDAO House London

## One line

**SagaDAO House is where Breakpoint's most useful conversations happen and are seen the same day, hosted with the care of a great London hotel.**

## What changes from Dubai

| Dubai | London |
|---|---|
| The house party was the product | **Programming is the product.** The party still happens. |
| Guests didn't know the plan, so they drifted | **Every guest knows tomorrow tonight.** They get a nightly briefing and a morning stand-up. |
| Free room, no expectations | **Free room in exchange for a signed content commitment** ([House Charter](guests/house-charter.md)) |
| Polished Day 1–7 films that landed too late | **Same-day, X-native content**, with one hero film afterwards |
| Content wasn't built to convert | **Every post points somewhere.** We track sponsor leads, followers and sign-ups. |
| No welcome, no gift, no speech | **Arrival is an event in itself** for residents and for all 300+ attendees |
| Sponsors paid for logos | **Sponsors own moments** (a session, a breakfast, the studio, the welcome) and get a results report |

## Principles

1. **Substance first.** Every session should be one a founder would leave the Breakpoint floor to attend. If we wouldn't clip it, we don't programme it.
2. **Nobody is ever unsure what's happening.** Each schedule, room, car and call time is communicated before anyone has to ask.
3. **Speed beats polish online.** We post a good clip within 2 hours instead of a perfect one a week later.
4. **Care is in the details.** Use people's names, know their coffee order, have a car waiting, put a handwritten note on the pillow. Small, specific and personal.
5. **Sponsors get outcomes, not impressions.** We sell participation and then prove results with numbers.
6. **Every moment is designed to be shared.** The door, the welcome kit, the studio set and the dinner table are all built to look good on camera.
7. **Safe by default.** No live location posts, only vetted staff, and door control at every event. Crypto guests are targets.

Set targets once the budget and sponsor deck are locked. Track them in `data/event.json` → `goals`.

| Inbound sponsor enquiries for the next house, within 30 days | Fixes "the event did nothing for future sponsors" | ≥ 10 |
| Programme attendance (unique check-ins) | Proves people came for the content | 300+ |
| Posts published during the week (house + guests) | Visibility | 150+ |
| Median time from moment to post | Speed | ≤ 2 h |
| Total impressions on X during the week | Reach | set after baseline |
| Follower growth on the SagaDAO account | Durable audience | set after baseline |
| Resident charter compliance | Guests delivered what they signed | 12 of 12 |
| Guest NPS (residents and attendees) | Felt premium | ≥ 70 |
