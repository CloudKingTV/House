# Nightly briefing

This fixes Dubai lesson #1. Nobody wakes up unsure what's happening.

- **Sent at 21:00** to the resident WhatsApp group by the house manager
- **Printed as a card** (A6, heavy stock) and left on each pillow at turndown
- Personalised lines (*"Your studio slot is 11:30"*) go as a direct message, not in the group
