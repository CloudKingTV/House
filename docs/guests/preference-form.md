# Resident preference form

Send it as a form (Tally, Typeform or Google Form) right after the charter is signed. Answers go into that guest's record in `data/guests.json`. **Medical notes stay out of the repo**; keep them only with the house manager and delete them after the event.

## Logistics
1. Full name (as you'd like it on your place card)
2. X handle
3. Arrival: date, time, airport, terminal, flight number
4. Departure: date, time, airport, flight number
5. Mobile number (with WhatsApp)
6. Emergency contact (name and number)

## Comfort
7. Dietary requirements
8. Allergies. *Anything we must tell the kitchen?*
9. Accessibility needs
10. Your coffee or tea order, exactly how you like it
11. Your drink of choice on arrival (alcoholic or not)
12. A snack you'd love to find in your room
13. Pillow: firm or soft?
14. Clothing size (T-shirt/hoodie) for your kit

## The week
15. What do you want to get out of this week?
16. **Who do you most want to meet during Breakpoint?** (people, projects or types of people)
17. Which House session would you like to host, speak at or moderate?
18. Anything you're launching or announcing that week?
19. Which London experience appeals most? (see [shortlist](../experiences/london-shortlist.md))
20. Is there a birthday, anniversary or milestone we should know about?
