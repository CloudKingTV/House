# The resident journey

What each resident experiences, from the first message to the last. Every step has an owner and a date, and each guest's progress is tracked on the dashboard's **Rooms** view.

## 1. Invitation (by 9 Oct)
- **Personal message first** (DM or call) from the host: "We'd love you in the house."
- **Then the formal invitation** as a designed PDF or email, with the dates, what the house is, the [charter](house-charter.md) and a 72-hour reply window.
- Once they accept and sign the charter, add them to `data/guests.json` with their room assignment and **send the [preference form](preference-form.md)**.

## 2. Pre-arrival dossier (sent 2 Nov)
A designed PDF, and a mobile-friendly page if possible:
- Welcome letter from the host
- The week at a glance ([schedule](../programme/schedule.md)), with their personal commitments highlighted
- Their room name, what's in the house, and Wi-Fi details on arrival
- **Their car:** pickup time, the driver's name and how they'll be met
- London in November: 6–12°C, rain likely, dark by 16:15. Pack a coat and proper shoes; we'll have umbrellas.
- The team: host, house manager and content lead, each with a WhatsApp number
- An invitation to the resident WhatsApp group

## 3. Before they land
- House manager confirms the flight 24 hours ahead and messages them: "[Driver] will be waiting at [terminal] with your name. See you soon."
- Room prepared from their preferences: pillow, drinks in the fridge, the snack they mentioned, and a **handwritten note from the host**.

## 4. Arrival (Sat 14)
1. The **driver** meets them at arrivals with a name board and has water, a charger and a small welcome card in the car.
2. The driver messages the house 10 minutes out.
3. **At the door**, the house manager greets them by name and hands them their drink of choice (from the preference form).
4. A **house tour** of 5 minutes, ending at their room.
5. In the room: the **welcome kit**, the note, and the printed week.
6. The content lead welcomes them at 17:30 for a 20-min onboarding.
7. **Welcome Dinner** at 19:30, with the [speech](../concierge/welcome-speech.md).

## 5. Every day
- **21:00** nightly briefing on WhatsApp ([template](daily-briefing-template.md)), plus a card on the pillow at turndown
- **09:15** stand-up: 10 minutes over breakfast covering the day, content assignments and any changes
- One house manager to message for anything, with a **15-minute response target**, 07:00–01:00
- Personal intros delivered from their "who I want to meet" list

## 6. Departure
- Checkout isn't a scramble: bags collected from the room, and the car confirmed the night before
- A **departure gift** plus a handwritten thank-you
- Water, snacks and a charger in the car

## 7. Afterwards
- **+24 h:** personal content pack (their photos and clips from the week) sent from the shared drive
- **+24 h:** a thank-you message from the host
- **+10 days:** charter reconciliation (hopefully just a thank-you)
- **+30 days:** first invite to the next house for anyone who went above and beyond
