# Shot list

A must-get list for the crew. Tick shots off on the day; missed shots get reassigned at the 09:15 stand-up.

## Arrival day (Sat 14)
- [ ] Welcome kit reveal, top-down, box opening (one resident, lit well)
- [ ] Key tag and handwritten note detail
- [ ] Resident reactions at the door. **Interior only; no street or front door.**
- [ ] Welcome Dinner: the table before guests sit, the speech, the toast
- [ ] Group portrait of all 12 residents (the week's anchor image)

## Every day
- [ ] Morning stand-up, 10 seconds of "the team getting ready"
- [ ] One conversation at Olympia per resident (they film, we collect)
- [ ] Studio: a wide shot of the set with the guest, plus the best 3 moments
- [ ] Every session: speaker close-ups, audience reactions, the best line
- [ ] Partner moment: their logo *in context* (on the set or on the table), never just a logo

## Flagship night
- [ ] The door moment: name check, wristband, drink in hand (with consent)
- [ ] The photo wall, throughout the night
- [ ] Crowd wide shots at the peak
- [ ] The toast
- [ ] Umbrellas going out into the rain, if it rains; it's a great shot

## Excursions
- [ ] Golden-hour group shot (before 15:45)
- [ ] Detail and texture shots (craft, food, city)

## Hero film coverage
- [ ] Establishing London shots: the Thames, rain on glass, black cabs, Olympia's façade
- [ ] Slow-motion moments, reserved for the hero film
- [ ] Short interviews: "What did this week mean to you?" (every resident, on Thursday)
