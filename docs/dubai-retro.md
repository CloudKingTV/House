# Dubai retro: what we learned from the last SagaDAO House

This is a living document. Whenever you remember something from Dubai, good or bad, add it under **Raw notes** and we'll turn it into a fix.

## The big five

### 1. Organisation: guests didn't know what was going on
**What happened:** Residents didn't have a clear picture of the day, so they made their own plans. The house stopped acting as a group, and we lost them for content and for sponsor moments.

**The fix in London:**
- A signed [House Charter](guests/house-charter.md) before flights are booked. Everyone knows the deal up front.
- A [pre-arrival dossier](guests/guest-journey.md#2-pre-arrival-dossier) sent on 2 Nov with the full week, room, cars and call times.
- A [nightly briefing](guests/daily-briefing-template.md) at 21:00 on WhatsApp, plus a printed card on the pillow at turndown.
- A 10-minute **morning stand-up** over breakfast (09:15) for content assignments and changes.
- One named house manager each guest can message for anything.

### 2. Commitment: free rooms with no expectations
**What happened:** Guests stayed for free but had no obligation to create content or join house moments.

**The fix:** The room is complimentary **in exchange for** a specific, countable content commitment. This is written into the charter, tracked daily on the dashboard, and billable at the stated nightly rate if it isn't met. See [House Charter](guests/house-charter.md).

### 3. Focus: all party, nothing to sponsor
**What happened:** The week was built around making the party happen. There was little that serious projects wanted to put their name on, or that attendees would cross town to see.

**The fix:** A real programme of sessions, builder breakfasts, a live studio, demo hour and closed-door roundtables, with each format built to be sponsored. The party still happens as the flagship night. See [Programme strategy](programme/strategy.md).

### 4. Production: slow, polished and didn't convert
**What happened:** The Day 1–7 films were professionally shot and edited but came out too late to ride the Breakpoint conversation. They didn't lead to anything measurable: no new sponsors, no pipeline.

**The fix:** A same-day content engine with an on-site edit bay, pre-built templates and posting targets (a moment within 2 h, a daily recap by 23:00). There is one hero film, delivered within 7 days. Every post has a job and a place to send people, and we measure sponsor enquiries, not only views. See [Content strategy](content/strategy.md).

### 5. Arrival: getting in didn't feel special
**What happened:** Residents got no welcome kit or welcome speech. Attendees got free food and drink but nothing that said *you're somewhere special*.

**The fix:**
- **Residents:** a chauffeur with a name board, a greeting by name at the door, a handwritten note, a curated [welcome kit](guests/welcome-kit.md), and a welcome dinner with a [speech](concierge/welcome-speech.md).
- **Attendees:** curated applications, an invitation-grade confirmation, a named welcome at the door, a drink in hand within 30 seconds, a take-home gift, and photos from the night in their inbox the next morning. See [Arrival experience](attendees/arrival-experience.md).

### 6. Budget: $55k spent on what should have cost $40k
**What happened:** It was our first official event, and costs we hadn't planned for kept appearing. There were props, the vehicle we wanted for transport, a karaoke machine one sponsor needed, and a sponsor asking for a refund partway through. Together those added about $5–10k, and the total came to roughly $55k against the $40k it should have cost.

**The fix:**
- **An envelope of $50–65k** for London, planned to the midpoint (see [budget](planning/budget.md)).
- **One budget owner.** Nothing is booked or paid without a line in `data/budget.json`.
- **A 15% contingency**, held back and spent only with a decision-log entry. First-event surprises cost 12–25%, so 10% wasn't enough.
- **Props and styling:** one list, locked by 30 Oct, with its own budget line. Nothing is bought off-list.
- **Transport:** cars booked by the trip for the airport and excursions. No special vehicle unless a partner pays for it.
- **Sponsor requests** (like the karaoke machine) are quoted and billed to the sponsor in advance, with a request deadline of 23 Oct ([terms](sponsors/terms.md)).
- **Refunds:** 50% on signing and the balance by 30 Oct. If a deliverable falls through, we make good with an equivalent, not cash. A live tracker shows sponsors what they're getting, so concerns are caught early ([terms](sponsors/terms.md)).
- **A weekly budget check** every Monday until the event, and daily during the week.
- **Written quotes with cancellation terms** before any deposit.
