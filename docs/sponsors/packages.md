# Sponsorship packages

**Draft pricing (USD), calibrated to the $50–65k budget.** All 12 slots total $74k, and the house breaks even at about 78% sell-through. Check it against what Dubai sponsors actually paid. The shift is from selling *logo placement* to selling **ownership of a moment, plus proof it worked**.

Every package includes:
- A named contact at SagaDAO and a kickoff call within 48 hours of signing
- A deliverables tracker shared with the sponsor
- **A post-event results report within 7 days**: attendance, content, impressions, and leads they can use ([template](../post-event/sponsor-report-template.md))
- A first-look option on the next SagaDAO House

## Presenting Partner · 1 slot · from $20,000
*"SagaDAO House London, presented by ___"*
- Name on the house: every invitation, the Luma page, signage and the X banner
- Named in the welcome speech and at the flagship night toast
- The headline session on Saga Sessions Night 1
- Hero item in the resident welcome kit, plus an insert in all 300 attendee gifts
- 4 House Studio episodes with their people
- Co-branded daily recap for the whole week
- 2 seats at the Welcome Dinner and Farewell Dinner
- Can invite 30 guests to the flagship night

## Programme Partner · 3 slots · from $8,000
Choose one to own: **Saga Sessions Night 1**, **Saga Sessions Night 2**, **Demo Hour**, or **the House Studio**.
- "___ presents" on the owned moment, and in every clip from it
- A speaker or host seat in the owned moment
- 2 House Studio episodes
- 6 dedicated clips posted from the house account, plus a recap thread
- Welcome kit inclusion
- Can invite 15 guests to the flagship night

## Experience Partner · 4 slots · from $5,000
Choose one to own: **a Builder Breakfast**, **a Closed Door roundtable**, **a Resident Dinner**, **the Welcome Kit**, **the Arrival Moment** (door, photo wall and gifts), or **a London excursion**.
- Hosts the moment; their founder gives the provocation or the toast
- 1 House Studio episode
- 3 dedicated clips plus a photo set
- Welcome kit inclusion
- Can invite 8 guests to the flagship night

## Supporter · 4 slots · from $2,500
- Logo on the partner wall and the Luma page
- Welcome kit or attendee gift inclusion
- 1 dedicated clip plus inclusion in the partner thank-you thread
- Can invite 4 guests to the flagship night

## Pipeline maths

| Tier | Slots | Price | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Presenting | 1 | $20,000 | $20,000 |
| Programme | 3 | $8,000 | $24,000 |
| Experience | 4 | $5,000 | $20,000 |
| Supporter | 4 | $2,500 | $10,000 |
| **Total** | **12** | | **$74,000** |

Planned cost is about **$58k**. Selling the Presenting slot plus 6 more roughly covers it, and everything beyond that is margin.

**In-kind counts too.** A partner who hosts a session in their own space, covers the flagship bar, or supplies the welcome kit can take an Experience or Programme slot for part-cash, part-in-kind. Record the in-kind value in their `valueUsd` and tag the budget line as partner-funded.

## What we won't sell

- Lanyards and logo walls with nothing attached
- Anything that puts a sponsor pitch in a resident's mouth without them agreeing to it
- Guest data. Sponsors get their own leads (people who came to their moment and opted in), never our full list.
