# Sponsorship packages

**Three tiers: $15k, $10k and $5k.** That's one step up from Dubai's $10k / $5k / $2.5k. The step up is justified by what's different this time: sponsors **own a moment**, get **same-day content** built around it, and receive **a results report** within a week.

Every package includes:
- A named contact at SagaDAO and a kickoff call within 48 hours of signing
- A deliverables tracker shared with the sponsor, so they can see progress at any time
- **A post-event results report within 7 days**: attendance, content, impressions, and their opted-in leads ([template](../post-event/sponsor-report-template.md))
- A first-look option on the next SagaDAO House

All packages are sold on the [sponsor terms](terms.md). Anything outside the package (equipment, props, extra activations) is quoted separately and paid by the sponsor.

## Founding Partner · 2 slots · $15,000
*"SagaDAO House London, with ___ and ___"*
- **Headlines one Saga Sessions night** (Night 1 or Night 2), with their exec on stage and "___ presents" on every clip from it
- **Co-presents the flagship night**: named at the door, on the screen and in the toast
- Named in the welcome speech; 2 seats at the Welcome Dinner and the Farewell Dinner
- 3 House Studio episodes with their people
- Hero item in the resident welcome kit, plus an insert in the attendee gift
- Logo on the invitation, the Luma page and the X banner for the week
- 8 dedicated clips plus a recap thread from @SagaMobileDAO
- Can invite 25 guests to the flagship night

## Programme Partner · 4 slots · $10,000
Choose one to own: **Demo Hour**, **the House Studio**, **a Builder Breakfast**, **a Closed Door roundtable**, or **the Welcome Dinner**.
- "___ presents" on the owned moment, and in every clip from it
- Their founder or exec hosts, judges or gives the provocation
- 2 House Studio episodes
- 5 dedicated clips plus a recap thread
- Welcome kit inclusion
- Can invite 12 guests to the flagship night

## Experience Partner · 6 slots · $5,000
Choose one to own: **a Resident Dinner**, **the Farewell Dinner**, **the Welcome Kit**, **the Arrival Moment** (door, photo wall and next-morning photos), **a London excursion**, **Office Hours**, or **the attendee gift**.
- Hosts or presents the moment
- 1 House Studio episode
- 3 dedicated clips plus a photo set
- Welcome kit or attendee gift inclusion
- Can invite 6 guests to the flagship night

## The maths

| Tier | Slots | Price | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Founding | 2 | $15,000 | $30,000 |
| Programme | 4 | $10,000 | $40,000 |
| Experience | 6 | $5,000 | $30,000 |
| **Total** | **12** | | **$100,000** |

Planned cost is about **$61k** (see [budget](../planning/budget.md)), so the house **breaks even at about 61% sold**. For example, 2 Founding + 3 Programme = $60k.

**If the higher prices meet resistance:**
- Hold the $15k tier firm; there are only 2 slots and they own the biggest moments.
- Offer an early-commit price (sign by 9 Oct: $12.5k / $8.5k / $4.5k). That rewards speed without discounting the package.
- **In-kind counts.** A partner who hosts a session in their space, covers the flagship bar, or supplies the welcome kit can pay partly in kind. Record the in-kind value in their `valueUsd` and tag the budget line as partner-funded.
- A smaller project can join as a **Friend of the House** for $2,500 (logo on the partner wall plus one clip), without taking one of the 12 slots.

## What we won't sell

- Lanyards and logo walls with nothing attached
- Anything that puts a sponsor pitch in a resident's mouth without them agreeing to it
- Guest data. Sponsors get their own leads (people who came to their moment and opted in), never our full list.
