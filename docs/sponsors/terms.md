# Sponsor terms

This is a plain-English terms sheet to attach to every sponsor agreement. It exists because of two Dubai lessons: **a sponsor asked for a refund**, and **a sponsor-specific request (a karaoke machine) came out of our budget**.

*This is not legal advice. Have a lawyer turn it into a short agreement before the first signing.*

---

## SagaDAO House London · Partner terms

**1. The package.** Your package is the list of deliverables in your order form, no more and no less. We'll share a live tracker so you can see each deliverable as it's delivered.

**2. Payment.**
- **50% on signing**, which secures your slot and your moment.
- **The remaining 50% by Fri 30 October 2026.**
- Invoiced in USD. We accept bank transfer or USDC (Solana); the wallet address is confirmed on the invoice and by a second channel.
- Deliverables before the event, such as logos on invitations and the Luma page, start once the first payment is received.

**3. Extras.** Anything beyond your package (equipment, props, extra activations, special requests) is **quoted in writing and paid by you in advance**. Requests for extras close on **Fri 23 October**, so we can source them properly.

**4. Assets and approvals.** Logos, kit items and approved messaging are due by **Fri 23 October**. We'll use pre-approved caption formats so content can go out the same day. If you want to approve each post, tell us at kickoff; posts awaiting approval can't be same-day.

**5. If you cancel.**
- **Before 23 October:** the first 50% is non-refundable; nothing further is due.
- **From 23 October:** the full fee is due. Your moment is already built, printed and promoted.

**6. If we don't deliver something.** If a deliverable can't happen as described (a speaker drops out, or the weather cancels an excursion), we **make good with an equivalent or better deliverable**, agreed with you. This covers things like an extra studio episode, additional clips or a different moment. **We don't give cash refunds for individual deliverables.**

**7. If the event can't happen.** If Breakpoint is cancelled or the House can't run for reasons outside our control, we'll refund what we haven't spent on your behalf, or roll your package into the next SagaDAO House, whichever you prefer.

**8. Your brand.** We use your name and logo only for the House and its content, and never in a way that implies endorsement beyond the partnership.

**9. Guests and data.** You receive opted-in leads from your own moment. We never share our full guest list.

---

## How we use this internally

- Record every extra a sponsor asks for as a budget line with `"partnerFunded": true` and a matching invoice. **If it isn't invoiced, it doesn't get bought.**
- Send the tracker link at kickoff and update it daily during the week. When a sponsor can see what they're getting, they're far less likely to ask for a refund.
- If a sponsor raises concerns, go straight to a make-good offer. Don't wait for the refund request.
