# London experiences: shortlist

For residents (and partner guests where it makes sense). Each is an idea to **check for availability, pricing and lead time**, because Breakpoint week is busy. Live status is in `data/experiences.json`.

**Planning notes for mid-November:**
- Daylight is **07:20–16:10**, so book outdoor or view experiences for the afternoon and shoot the content by 15:45.
- Expect rain. Every outdoor plan needs an indoor alternative.
- The best days are **Wed 18** and **Thu 19**, after the conference.
- Offer **two options per slot**, so people choose one and don't wander off.

## Signature experiences

| Experience | Why it's memorable | Content | Lead time | Sponsorable |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Private Thames boat at dusk** | London's skyline by water; the sunset lands at the perfect time | Excellent: golden hour and group shots | 2–3 weeks | ✔ |
| **Savile Row bespoke visit and fitting** | A genuinely rare experience; the tailoring house explains the craft | Strong: craft, detail and texture | 2–4 weeks | ✔ |
| **Private dining room / chef's table** for the Farewell Dinner | A proper send-off | Strong | 3–4 weeks | ✔ |
| **Ceremony of the Keys**, Tower of London (nightly, 21:30) | A 700-year-old ritual; very few people ever see it | No filming allowed, which makes it more special | Tickets sell out months ahead; check now | |
| **Horizon 22 or Sky Garden** | The highest free views in London | Strong in daylight | Book when slots are released | |

## How we run excursions
- One host from the team goes on every excursion.
- **Cars at the door**; nobody has to navigate.
- One shooter or the photographer comes along, and it's part of the day's content plan.
- Share the group photo in the resident group within an hour.
