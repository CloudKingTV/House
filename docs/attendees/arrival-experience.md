# Attendee arrival experience

For the 300+ people who come to sessions and the flagship night. In Dubai, getting in felt like nothing special. In London, **the experience starts before they arrive** and continues after they leave.

## 1. Applying: curated, not open
- **Luma with approval required.** Asking "What are you building?" and "Your X handle" gives us a curated room and makes acceptance feel earned.
- Capacity per event, with a waitlist. **Scarcity is part of the appeal**, so don't overbook the venue.
- Consent line: *"Photos and video will be taken. Ask for a no-photo wristband at the door."*
- Partners get invite allocations (see [packages](../sponsors/packages.md)) through their own Luma codes, so we can attribute who brought whom.

## 2. Confirmation: invitation-grade
- The acceptance email is **designed like an invitation**, not a transactional note: the house mark, their name, the date, and one line on what to expect.
- A personal QR code for check-in.
- **Day before:** a reminder with the time, arrival window, what's on, dress, and "it's November in London, and we have a cloakroom".
- **Two hours before:** "Doors at 19:00. See you shortly."

## 3. The door: 30 seconds to "wow"
| Seconds | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0 | A host with a tablet finds their name. **"Welcome, [first name]."** No list confusion. |
| 10 | Wristband or pin on. Colour codes: guest, partner, speaker, resident, no-photo. |
| 15 | Coat and umbrella taken by the cloakroom. |
| 30 | **Drink in hand**, from a server positioned right inside the door. |
| 60 | The **arrival moment**: a lit photo wall or set with the house photographer. Photos are delivered the next morning. |

**Ops:**
- Two check-in hosts per 150 expected guests, with staggered arrival windows in the invitation (19:00, 19:30, 20:00).
- An SIA door supervisor alongside the hosts, not in front of them. Security should feel like hospitality.
- A covered queue or awning if it rains.
- VIP and partner lane, which is also used for speakers and residents.

## 4. Inside
- **Something to learn and something to do.** Sessions start on time, and the programme is visible (printed and on a screen).
- Food that's plated or passed, not a buffet scrum.
- Hosts on the floor, who know the partners, make introductions and look after anyone standing alone.

## 5. Leaving
- **Gift at the exit**, not the entrance, so no one carries it all night ([options](../guests/welcome-kit.md#attendee-gift-300)).
- Umbrellas handed out if it's raining.
- Cars or a cab rank arranged for late finishes.

## 6. The next morning
- **An email from the house by 11:00:** "Thanks for coming. Here are your photos." It links the photo gallery, the session clips and the X thread, and it's the moment we ask them to follow and share.
- Include one line in the email for partners: *"Want your project involved in the next SagaDAO House? Reply to this email."* This is conversion, built into the thank-you.
